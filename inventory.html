The Inventory

A comprehensive checklist of everything you should track. Use this as a reference, then download the template to create your own list.

Download the Template

Email & Communication

These are often the master keys to every other account. If your executor can access email, they can reset most other passwords.

  • Gmail / Google Workspace
  • Outlook / Hotmail / Microsoft 365
  • Yahoo Mail
  • AOL Mail
  • ProtonMail
  • iCloud Mail
  • Other email providers

Financial Accounts

Banking, investments, payment apps, and digital currencies.

  • Primary bank (checking / savings)
  • Secondary banks
  • Credit cards (online portals)
  • Investment accounts (Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard, E*Trade)
  • Retirement accounts (401k, IRA)
  • PayPal
  • Venmo
  • Zelle
  • Apple Pay / Google Pay
  • Cryptocurrency exchanges (Coinbase, Binance, Kraken)
  • Cryptocurrency wallets (hardware wallets, software wallets)
  • Credit monitoring (Credit Karma, Experian)

Social Media

Decide for each: memorialize, delete, or transfer?

  • Facebook
  • Instagram
  • X (Twitter)
  • LinkedIn
  • Pinterest
  • Reddit
  • Nextdoor
  • TikTok
  • Snapchat
  • YouTube (if separate from Google)

Cloud Storage & Photos

Where your documents, photos, and videos live.

  • Google Drive & Google Photos
  • iCloud
  • Dropbox
  • OneDrive
  • Amazon Photos
  • Flickr
  • Shutterfly
  • External hard drives / NAS (network storage)

Shopping & Retail

  • Amazon
  • eBay
  • Etsy
  • Walmart
  • Target
  • Costco
  • Other retail accounts with stored payment methods

Subscriptions & Entertainment

  • Netflix
  • Hulu
  • Disney+
  • Max (HBO)
  • Amazon Prime Video
  • Apple TV+
  • Spotify
  • Apple Music
  • YouTube Premium
  • Audible
  • Kindle / Amazon Books
  • Newspaper / magazine subscriptions

Travel & Loyalty Programs

  • Airline frequent flyer (Delta, United, American, Southwest)
  • Hotel loyalty (Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, IHG)
  • Airbnb
  • VRBO
  • Expedia / Booking.com
  • Uber
  • Lyft
  • Car rental (Hertz, Enterprise, Avis, National)
  • TSA PreCheck / Global Entry

Healthcare

  • Patient portals (MyChart, Epic, etc.)
  • Medicare.gov
  • Health insurance portals
  • Pharmacy accounts (CVS, Walgreens, mail-order)
  • Telehealth services
  • Fitness trackers / health apps (Fitbit, Apple Health)

Government & Legal

  • Social Security Administration (SSA.gov)
  • IRS (for tax transcripts and records)
  • State DMV / driver's license
  • Online will services (LegalZoom, Trust & Will)
  • Veterans Affairs (VA.gov)

Smart Home & IoT

These control physical access to your home, property, and vehicle. Often overlooked, but critical.

  • WiFi router / mesh network (Eero, Orbi, etc.)
  • Smart locks (August, Yale, Schlage)
  • Security systems (Ring, Nest Secure, SimpliSafe, ADT)
  • Video doorbells (Ring, Nest, Arlo)
  • Smart thermostats (Nest, Ecobee, Honeywell)
  • Smart lights (Philips Hue, LIFX)
  • Voice assistants (Alexa, Google Home, Siri)
  • Smart garage door openers
  • Home automation hubs (SmartThings, Hubitat)
  • Smart vehicle apps (Tesla, FordPass, GM, BMW Connected) — unlock, locate, remote start; no known legacy policies

AI & Emerging Services

New category. Most of these have no legacy or death policies yet.

  • ChatGPT / OpenAI
  • Claude (Anthropic)
  • Gemini (Google)
  • Microsoft Copilot
  • Perplexity
  • Midjourney / DALL-E / image generators
  • Custom GPTs or AI projects you've built

Devices & Hardware

Your executor needs to know what exists and how to unlock it.

  • Smartphone(s) — with passcode / PIN / biometric
  • Tablet(s)
  • Laptop(s) / desktop computer(s)
  • External hard drives
  • Network-attached storage (NAS)
  • USB backup drives
  • Hardware security keys (YubiKey, etc.)

Websites, Domains & Online Business

  • Domain registrars (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google Domains)
  • Web hosting accounts
  • WordPress / Wix / Squarespace sites
  • Online stores (Shopify, Etsy seller)
  • Email hosting

Other Accounts

  • Online gaming (Steam, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo)
  • Dating apps
  • Classifieds (Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace seller)
  • Professional associations
  • Alumni networks
  • Volunteer organizations
Don't expect to check every box. Most people have 30–50 accounts, not 100+. Start with the ones that matter most: email, financial, and anything that controls access to your home.

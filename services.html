Workshops & Workbook

Sometimes you need more than a website. Our guided workshops and printable workbook walk you through the process with hands-on help.

Guided Workshops

A 2-hour guided session where we walk through the entire digital estate planning process together. You'll leave with a completed inventory, a chosen digital executor, and platform legacy features turned on.

What We Cover

  • Understanding your digital footprint — what you have and where it lives
  • Creating a complete account inventory using our template
  • Choosing and preparing your digital executor
  • Setting up a password manager with emergency access
  • Configuring Google, Apple, and Facebook legacy features
  • Documenting everything for your will or trust
  • Smart home and IoT considerations
  • Passkeys and modern authentication — what your family needs to know

Who It's For

  • Seniors who want to get their digital affairs in order
  • Adult children helping aging parents organize their accounts
  • Couples who want to ensure mutual access and clarity
  • Anyone who's been putting this off and needs accountability

Format Options

In-Person Workshop

Small group session (6–12 people) at a community center, library, or senior center. Hands-on with devices and accounts.

Duration: 2 hours
Includes: Printed workbook, inventory template, follow-up email summary

Private Session

One-on-one or family session at your home or via video call. Personalized to your specific accounts and devices.

Duration: 2–3 hours
Includes: Customized inventory, executor coaching, platform setup assistance

Organization / Company

For employee wellness programs, HOA groups, retirement communities, or professional associations.

Duration: Flexible
Includes: Customized content, materials for all attendees, Q&A

No tech expertise required. We explain everything in plain language and work at your pace. Bring your phone, tablet, or laptop — we'll use what you're comfortable with.

The Workbook

A printable, self-guided workbook that expands on the free website content with exercises, conversation prompts, and detailed walkthroughs.

What's Inside

  • Step-by-step exercises for each of the 5 planning steps
  • Conversation guides for talking to your digital executor and family
  • Expanded inventory templates with space for 100+ accounts
  • Platform-specific checklists for Google, Apple, Facebook, Microsoft, and more
  • Smart home device tracker — list every IoT device, app, and account
  • Passkey planning worksheet — document where your passkeys live and how to access them
  • Emergency info card templates — wallet-sized cards for your family
  • Review schedule — reminders to update your plan every 6–12 months

Format

  • Print edition: Spiral-bound, 80 pages, large print, writeable worksheets
  • Digital edition: PDF with fillable forms, usable on tablet or computer
  • Bundle: Both print and digital, plus the emergency card laminates
